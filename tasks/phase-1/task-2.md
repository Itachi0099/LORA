Assemble 20 recorded mixes with deliberate spread across genre and blend style.

## Steps

- Prefer full-length recordings (20–180 min).
- Store on disk; record path + `sha256` + duration per the 1.1 schema.

## Done when

- Genre/blend spread is visibly balanced, not skewed to one style.
